Surface Environment Index

Temperature, water phase, pressure and atmosphere assumptions only. Even 100/100 does not imply Earth-like gravity, habitability, biological viability or human safety.

What goes into this score?

    Temperature: 40 points; exposed liquid water: 25; pressure: 20; atmosphere: 15. Airless worlds score zero. Gas envelopes have no surface score. Gravity, density, radiation, toxicity and biological viability are not evaluated. Seasonal extremes, atmospheric escape and geological evolution are not simulated.

    Earth Similarity Index

    Similarity in radius, density, escape velocity and modeled temperature. Similarity does not establish habitability.

    Evidence, assumptions & model limits

      Energy balance and a pressure-scaled greenhouse offset estimate a mean temperature; they do not resolve weather or seasons. Stellar size and luminosity are representative unless catalogued. Tidal state, geothermal activity and a biosphere remain unknown.
